# Sinclair ZX Spectrum / ZX81 / Timex Cassette Interface Specification

## 1. Overview
The **Sinclair ZX80, ZX81, ZX Spectrum (16K, 48K, 128K, +2, +3)** and **Timex Sinclair 1000/2068** use a standardized 3.5 mm dual-jack audio tape interface for program storage (*LOAD / SAVE*).

## 2. Audio Jack Pinouts

### EAR Socket (Tape Master -> Computer / LOAD)
- **Jack Type:** 3.5 mm mono/stereo jack (`J2`)
- **Tip (T):** `SPECTRUM_EAR` signal
- **Ring (R):** Connected to Tip (accommodates both mono and stereo patch cables)
- **Sleeve (S):** Ground (0V reference)
- **Signal Level:** ~0.7 V to 1.5 Vpp AC coupled square wave pulses.

### MIC Socket (Computer -> Tape Master / SAVE)
- **Jack Type:** 3.5 mm mono/stereo jack (`J3`)
- **Tip (T):** `SPECTRUM_MIC` signal
- **Ring (R):** Connected to Tip
- **Sleeve (S):** Ground (0V reference)
- **Signal Level:** ~0.5 V to 1.0 Vpp pulses generated by Sinclair ULA port `0xFE`.

## 3. Tape Master Interface Architecture
1. **Bus Connection (`J1`)**: Universal 40-pin right-angle female socket (`PinSocket_2x20_P2.54mm_Horizontal`), Pin 26 hardwired to GND (`CART_DETECT_N`).
2. **Dual 3.5 mm Ports (`J2`, `J3`)**: Through-hole audio jacks (`Connector_Audio:Jack_3.5mm_CUI_SJ1-3523N_Horizontal`).
3. **Bench Test Header (`J4`)**: 6-pin 2.54 mm right-angle pin-header in parallel with all lines (`GND`, `+5V`, `EAR`, `MIC`, `REM_A`, `REM_B`).
4. **Smart Auto-ID EEPROM (`U1`)**: 24C02 on I2C bus identifying Cartridge ID `#04`.
5. **Level Buffering & Conditioning**:
   - `EAR` Output: Buffered with 74HCT06 open-collector inverter stage, attenuated via precision voltage divider to ~1.2 Vpp and AC-coupled into the Spectrum EAR port.
   - `MIC` Input: Conditioned with high-speed transistor saturating stage driving `TAPE_REC_IN` (3.3V TTL), and AC-coupled to `AUDIO_RAW_L` for real-time VU-meter and analog digitizer on the Mainboard.
   - `REM` Control: Dual MOSFET / solid-state relay switch `Q1`/`Q2` (BSS138) driven by `MOTOR_CTRL` and monitored by `MOTOR_SENSE`.
6. **ESD Protection (`D1`, `D2`)**: USBLC6-2SC6 TVS arrays protecting all externally exposed audio lines.
